# Services

An example price list for an example consultancy. Replace it with your own.
The speed to lead proposal can only offer what is written here, at these prices.

## Automation audit

Two weeks. We map how the work is done today, find the three processes that cost the most time, and
estimate what automating each one would save. Fixed price: £3,000.

## Build one automation

Four to six weeks. We build, test and deploy one automation in your own cloud account, with logging,
alerts and a handover session. From £12,000, quoted after an audit or a scoping call.

## Support

Monthly. We watch your automations, fix what breaks and make small changes. £1,500 a month for up to
five automations.

## Not something we do

Websites, mobile apps, hardware, and staff training on its own.
